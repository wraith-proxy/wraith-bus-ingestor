Wraith Bus tracks live buses across Great Britain, pulled straight from
the UK Bus Open Data Service (BODS) every 60 seconds and served by a
single self-hosted backend.

Two screens:

Tracking -- a live map of every bus currently reporting a position, with
a search bar for localities and lines, and route overlays on request.
The basemap is self-hosted OpenStreetMap vector tiles.

Dashboard -- pipeline health and punctuality: feed freshness, data
quality test results, and system-wide on-time statistics for the
underlying ETA models.

This is not a general-purpose bus tracker you can point at any backend.
It ships built against one specific self-hosted server, and the whole
stack -- ingestor, database, API, and this app -- is a single open
source project (MIT licensed).

No accounts, no analytics, and the app never reads the device's own
location -- it only ever displays where the buses are.

Optional push notifications relay pipeline alerts (for example, the feed
going stale) from a self-hosted ntfy topic. A build with no topic
configured simply never starts that feature -- no crash, nothing broken.

Not distributed via the Google Play Store. Sideload the APK directly, or
